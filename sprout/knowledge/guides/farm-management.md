# Farm Management (for farmers)

Farm Management is for accounts with the **Farmer** role. Open it from the **Farm Management** menu item.

## Register your first farm

1. Open **Farm Management**. If you have no farm yet you will see "No Farms Registered".
2. Click **Register Farm**.
3. Enter the **Farm Name** (for example Green Valley Farms), the **Land Size (acres)** and the **Location (District)**.
4. Click **Create Farm**. Your farm appears as a card.

## Add more farms

Click **Add Farm** at the top right. If you have several farms, each one gets its own tab; click a tab to switch farms.

## Add crops

1. Select the farm.
2. Under **Current Crops**, click **Add Crop**.
3. Fill in **Crop Name** (for example Paddy, Chilli, Maize), **Variety** (for example BG 360), **Area (acres)**, **Planted Date**, **Expected Yield (kg)** and **Status**.
4. Click **Add Crop** in the form. The crop shows in the Current Crops table, where you can edit it (the button becomes **Update Crop**) or delete it.

Crop status can be planned, growing, harvested or failed. The "Active Crops" number on your Dashboard counts crops that are growing.

## Record expenses and revenue

- Click **Add Expense** and enter the **Category**, **Amount (LKR)**, **Date** and an optional **Description**.
- Click **Record Revenue** and enter the **Revenue Source** (for example Paddy), the **Total Amount (LKR)** and the **Date**.

They appear under **Recent Expenses** and **Revenue Records**.

## Farm insights

The insights panel shows **Total Revenue**, **Total Expenses**, **Net Profit** and **Profit Margin** for the selected farm.

## Edit or delete a farm

Use the **Edit** and **Delete** buttons on the farm card (after editing, click **Update Farm**). Deleting a farm removes its crops, expenses and revenue records and cannot be undone.

## Your Dashboard numbers

On the Dashboard, "Acres Farming" is the total land size of your farms and "Active Crops" is the number of growing crops.
