# Learning Hub (courses)

The Learning Hub has free courses taught by agricultural experts. Open it from the **Learning Hub** menu item. You can browse the courses without an account; you need to sign in to enrol.

## Find a course

1. Open **Learning Hub**.
2. Use the search box ("Search by title, category, or instructor...") to find a course.
3. Use **Category** to filter by topic such as Crop Management, Soil Science, Pest Control, Irrigation, Organic Farming or Agri-Business.
4. Use **Sort By** to order courses, for example shortest first or longest first.

Each course card shows the level (Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced), the length in hours, the rating, the instructor and how many students are enrolled.

## Enrol and learn

1. Click **View Course** on a card to open its details.
2. Click **Enroll Now - Free**. Courses cost nothing.
3. The course page opens with a video player. Pick a lesson from the **Course Videos** list on the right; the lessons play in order.
4. Open the **Notes** tab to write notes for the video you are watching. Notes are only kept while the page is open; they are not saved to your account yet. Open the **Materials** tab to download the reading materials for the course (click **Download** next to an item).

After you enrol, the course shows **Enrolled**. Your finished courses are counted as "Courses Completed" on your Dashboard.

## Courses with no videos yet

Some courses have no lessons or downloads yet. The page says "No videos available yet" or "No reading materials available". The instructor adds them over time.

## For agricultural experts

If your account role is **Agricultural Expert**, you can create courses from the Learning Hub: add a title, description, category, level, duration in hours, a thumbnail image link, lessons as YouTube links and downloadable materials as web links. You can edit or delete your own courses at any time.
