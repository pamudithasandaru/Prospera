# Weather Portal

The Weather page shows live weather and a forecast for every district of Sri Lanka. Open it from the **Weather** menu item. You can use it without an account.

## Choose your district

1. Open **Weather**.
2. Use the **District** menu at the top right to pick one of the 25 districts, for example Kandy, Jaffna, Galle or Anuradhapura.
3. If you are signed in and your profile has a district, the page starts with that district. Your last choice is remembered on this device.

## What you see

- **Current Weather**: temperature, condition, humidity, wind speed, rainfall and pressure right now.
- **10-Day Forecast**: for each day the highest and lowest temperature, the expected rainfall and the strongest wind.
- **Temperature Trend**: a chart of the daily highs and lows.
- **Agricultural Insights**: advice for **Planting Conditions**, **Irrigation Advisory** and **Pest and Disease Risk**, worked out from the forecast for your district.
- **Advisories** at the top: warnings such as heavy rain, thunderstorms, strong wind, heat or a dry spell, when the forecast calls for them.

## How reliable is it?

- The weather figures come from Open-Meteo or MET Norway, free weather services. The page names the one that was used at the bottom, with the time of the last update.
- The advisories and insights are generated automatically from the forecast. They are general guidance, not official warnings. Always check your own fields and follow official announcements from the Department of Meteorology.

## If the page says the weather is unavailable

The weather service may be busy. Click **Retry** or try again in a few minutes.
