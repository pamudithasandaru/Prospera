# AI Tools Lab: Plant Disease Detection

The AI Tools Lab checks a photo of a plant leaf for disease. It is available to accounts with the **Farmer** or **Agricultural Expert** role. Open it from the **AI Tools Lab** menu item.

## Check a leaf

1. Open **AI Tools Lab**. The page is titled "AI Plant Disease Detection".
2. In **Upload Crop Image**, drag a photo into the box or click it, or click **Choose Image**. JPG, PNG and WebP files up to 25 MB are accepted.
3. Check the preview, then click **Analyse Image**.
4. Wait for **Analysis Results** to appear on the right.

## Get a good result

- Photograph the affected leaves clearly, in good natural light.
- Fill the frame with one leaf instead of a whole field.
- Avoid blurry, dark or heavily filtered photos.

## What the result means

The tool recognises four conditions: **Healthy**, **Rust**, **Scab** and **Multiple Diseases**. The result shows the most likely condition with a confidence figure and a **Recommended Treatment** list.

- A low confidence (for example under 50 percent) means the tool is unsure. Try a clearer photo from another angle.
- The result is guidance only, not a diagnosis. For a severe outbreak, contact a local agriculture officer.

## If the analysis fails

The analysis runs on a separate service that can take a while to start after a quiet period. If you see "ML service request failed" or "Prediction failed", wait a minute and click **Analyse Image** again. If it keeps failing, create a ticket in **Support**.
