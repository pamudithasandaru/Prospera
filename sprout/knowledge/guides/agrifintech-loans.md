# AgriFinTech: Credit Score and Loans

AgriFinTech is for accounts with the **Farmer** role. Open it from the **AgriFinTech** menu item.

## What the page shows

- **Your Credit Score**: your score and its rating (for example Good), with the date it was last updated.
- **Total Borrowed**: the total amount of your loans.
- **Pending Applications**: how many loan applications are waiting for a decision.
- **My Loans**: a table of all your loan applications.
- **Financial Tips**: advice such as how to improve your credit score.

## Apply for an agricultural loan

1. Open **AgriFinTech**.
2. Click **Apply for Loan** (or **Apply for Your First Loan** if you have none yet).
3. In the **Apply for Agricultural Loan** form enter the **Loan Amount (LKR)**, the **Purpose** (for example buying seeds or a water pump) and the **Loan Duration**.
4. Click **Submit Application**.

Your application appears in **My Loans** with the status **pending**.

## Loan statuses

- **pending**: waiting for review.
- **approved**: accepted.
- **active**: the loan is running.
- **rejected**: not accepted.
- **closed**: fully repaid or finished.

## Tips

- Pay instalments on time to keep your credit score healthy.
- Only apply for the amount you need and can repay.
- Questions about a decision? Open **Support** and click **Create Ticket**.

Sprout cannot see your own loans or credit score, because those are private. Check them on this page.
