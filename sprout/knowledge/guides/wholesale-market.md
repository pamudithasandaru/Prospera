# Wholesale Market

The Wholesale Market shows produce listings so farmers and buyers can see what is available and at what price. Open it from the **Wholesale Market** menu item. You can browse it without an account.

## Browse and search listings

1. Open **Wholesale Market**.
2. Type in the search box ("Search products...") to find a product by name.
3. Use the **Category** menu to show only one type: Vegetables, Fruit, Grain, Dairy or Spice. Choose **All Categories** to see everything.
4. Click **More Filters** to open the extra filter options.

## Understanding a listing

Each listing card shows:

- the product name and category,
- the **price per unit** (for example LKR 120 per litre),
- the **available quantity**,
- the **district** where the produce is,
- whether the seller is **verified** by the platform (a green tick).

Click **View Details** to open the full description, price and available quantity. Click **Close** to go back.

## Buying and contacting sellers

The **Inquire** and **Contact Seller** buttons are shown on listings, but direct messaging to sellers from the Wholesale Market is not active yet. To ask about a listing today, open **Support**, click **Create Ticket** and mention the product name so the support team can help.

## Selling your produce

Farmers cannot yet add their own listings from the website. Wholesale listings are added and verified by the platform team. If you want your produce listed, create a ticket in the **Support** page with your product, quantity, price and district.

## Prices

Prices and quantities change. Always check the listing on the page for the latest figures. When Sprout quotes a price it comes from the last update of the catalogue and shows the date.
