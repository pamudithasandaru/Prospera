# Haritha Bhoomi (හරිත භූමි) - Platform Overview

Haritha Bhoomi is an agriculture platform for Sri Lankan farmers, buyers and agricultural experts. Its tagline is "Grow, Learn, Share". Everything is available on phones, tablets and computers.

## What you can do

- **Farm Management**: register your farms, track crops, expenses and revenue (farmers).
- **Wholesale Market**: browse produce listings with price, quantity and district.
- **Marketplace**: browse farm equipment such as tractors, sprayers and irrigation systems and add items to your cart.
- **Learning Hub**: free courses from agricultural experts.
- **Government Portal**: find subsidies, loans, training and insurance schemes and apply (farmers).
- **Weather**: live weather and a 10-day forecast for every district, with farming advice.
- **AI Tools Lab**: upload a leaf photo to check for plant disease (farmers and experts).
- **AgriFinTech**: your credit score and agricultural loan applications (farmers).
- **AgriLink Social**: a community feed, groups, stories and private messages.
- **Support**: frequently asked questions and support tickets.
- **Sprout**: this assistant. Ask it about farming, products, courses, schemes and how to use the website.

## Who can open what

- **Without an account** you can browse the Wholesale Market, Marketplace, Learning Hub, Government Portal, Weather and the social feed.
- **You need to sign in** to add to cart, enrol in a course, apply for a scheme, post, message, open your Dashboard or contact Support.
- **Farmers** also get Farm Management, AI Tools Lab and AgriFinTech.
- **Buyers** get the Wholesale Market and Marketplace for trading.
- **Agricultural experts** get the AI Tools Lab and can create courses in the Learning Hub.
- **Admins** (the platform team) manage users, content and the chatbot.

## Finding your way around

On a computer the main pages are in the bar at the top. If there are more pages than fit, open the **More** menu at the end of the bar. On a phone, tap the menu button (three lines) at the top left. The search icon, messages icon, notification bell and your picture are at the top right. Your picture opens your profile and the Logout option.

## Getting help

Ask Sprout using the green chat button at the bottom right of any page, or open **Support** and click **Create Ticket**.
