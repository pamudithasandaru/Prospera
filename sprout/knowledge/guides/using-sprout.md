# Using Sprout, the Haritha Bhoomi Assistant

Sprout is the chat assistant of Haritha Bhoomi. It answers questions about farming, the products and courses on the platform, government schemes and how to use the website.

## Start a chat

1. Click the green chat button at the bottom right of any page.
2. Type your question and press Enter or the send arrow, or tap one of the suggested questions.
3. Use the restart button at the top of the chat to begin a new conversation.

## Things you can ask

- **Farming advice** about drip irrigation, organic pest control, soil health and similar topics.
- **Products**: the price, stock and category of a Marketplace item.
- **Wholesale market**: which produce is listed, at what price and in which district.
- **Courses**: which courses exist for a topic, and their level and length.
- **Government schemes**: what a scheme gives and who is eligible.
- **How to use the website**: step-by-step help with adding a crop, applying for a loan, choosing your weather district and more.

Ask in English, Sinhala or Tamil. Sprout answers in the language you write in when it can.

## How its answers work

- Sprout answers only from the information it has been given: the platform's products, wholesale listings, courses, government schemes, how-to guides and farming guides.
- Product, listing, course and scheme details are copied from the platform regularly. When Sprout quotes a price, stock level or quantity it tells you the date of the information. Prices can change after that, so open the page it points to for the latest.
- Answers show where they came from. If Sprout does not know, it says so instead of guessing; then try the **Support** page.

## What Sprout cannot do

- It cannot see anyone's private information: your account, orders, messages, finances, loans or applications.
- It cannot buy, apply, enrol or change anything for you. It tells you which page and buttons to use.
- Its farming advice is general. For a severe pest or disease outbreak, contact your local agriculture officer.
