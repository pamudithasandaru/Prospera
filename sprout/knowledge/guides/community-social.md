# AgriLink Social (community)

AgriLink Social is the farmer community: share posts, ask questions, follow topics and message other members. Open it from the **AgriLink Social** menu item. You can read the feed without an account; posting, reacting, connecting and messaging need you to sign in.

## The feed

Use the tabs above the feed to choose what you see:

- **For You**: recommended posts.
- **Following**: posts from farmers you are connected to.
- **Trending**: the most liked and commented discussions.
- **Q&A**: questions and farming problems, including pest questions.
- **Stories**: harvest milestones and success stories.
- **Saved**: posts you bookmarked.

Use the search box to find posts by keyword, crop, topic or farmer name. Click a hashtag such as #paddy or #harvest on a post to show only posts with that tag. A banner shows your active search or tag; remove it to see everything again.

## Write a post

1. At the top of the feed use the post box and its **Photo**, **Video** or **Article** buttons.
2. Write your text. You can add hashtag suggestions such as #organic or #irrigation and emojis. For an article, add a title as well.
3. Publish it. If the form offers a choice of who can see the post, pick the audience you want.

## Join in

- **React** to a post with Like, Insightful or Celebrate.
- **Comment**, and reply to a comment; replying tags the author automatically.
- **Share** a post: your device's share sheet opens, or the link is copied.
- **Save** a post with the bookmark button to find it again in **Saved** (saved posts are kept on the device you use).
- Create a **story** from the stories bar at the top, and tap a story to watch it.

## Meet farmers

Click a farmer's name or picture to open a quick preview of their location, farm and crops. From there you can **Connect**, **Message** or **View Full Profile**. The left side of the feed has shortcuts to **My Network**, **My Groups** and **Messages**.

## Private messages

Open **Messages** from the speech-bubble icon at the top right. Use **Search conversations** to find a chat and type in **Type a message** to reply. You need to be connected to someone to start a chat.

## Staying safe

Use the menu on a post to **Save**, **Copy Link**, **Report** or, on your own posts, **Delete**. Report posts that break the community rules.
