# Marketplace (farm equipment)

The Marketplace sells farm machinery and equipment such as tractors, harvesters, irrigation systems and sprayers. Open it from the **Marketplace** menu item. You can browse without an account.

## Browse and search

1. Open **Marketplace**.
2. Type in the search box ("Search equipment...") to find an item by name.
3. Use the **Category** menu to choose Tractors, Harvesters, Irrigation Systems, Sprayers, Hand Tools or Processing Equipment, or **All Categories**.
4. Click **More** to show extra filters such as **Low Stock** and **Out of Stock**.

## Reading a product card

A product card shows the name, category, a short description, the price (and the regular price if it is discounted), how many are in stock and whether shipping is free. Items that are out of stock cannot be added to your cart.

## Add an item to your cart

1. Sign in first. Adding to the cart needs an account, and you will be asked to sign in if you are not.
2. Click **Add to Cart** on the product.
3. The number on the cart icon at the top of the page goes up.

Your cart is saved to your account. An online cart page and checkout are not available yet, so you cannot pay on the website at the moment. For help buying an item, open **Support** and click **Create Ticket** with the product name.

## Prices and stock

Prices and stock levels change. The product card on the page always shows the latest. When Sprout quotes a price it shows the date of the catalogue it learned from.
