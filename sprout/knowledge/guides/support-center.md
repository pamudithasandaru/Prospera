# Support Center

The Support Center is where you ask the platform team for help. Open it from the **Support** menu item. You need to be signed in.

## Create a support ticket

1. Open **Support** (the page is titled "Community Support Center").
2. Under **My Support Tickets**, click **Create Ticket**.
3. Fill in the **Subject**, choose a **Category** and describe the problem in **Description**.
4. Submit the form. Your ticket appears in the table.

Use a ticket for problems with your account, an order or listing question, a loan or scheme application that looks wrong, or anything on the website that does not work.

## Track your tickets

The cards at the top count your **Open Tickets**, **Resolved** and **Pending** tickets. The table shows each ticket's ID, subject, category, status and creation date. The statuses are:

- **open**: received and waiting for the team.
- **pending**: the team is working on it.
- **resolved**: answered or fixed.
- **closed**: finished.

## Frequently asked questions

The page has an FAQ section with short answers about registering a farm, applying for government schemes, AI disease detection and loan applications. Check it before creating a ticket.

## Emergency hotlines

The page also lists **Emergency Hotlines** and a **Hotline Support** option for urgent situations.

## When Sprout cannot help

Sprout only knows public information about the platform and farming. It cannot see your account, orders, messages or applications. For anything personal, create a ticket here.
