# Government Portal (schemes and subsidies)

The Government Portal lists subsidies, low-interest loans, training programmes and insurance schemes. Open it from the **Government Portal** menu item. You can browse the schemes without an account; you need to sign in to apply.

## Browse schemes

1. Open **Government Portal**.
2. The top of the page shows counts such as **Active Schemes**, **Approved Applications**, **Pending Review** and **New Advisories**.
3. Scroll through the scheme cards. Each shows the scheme name, its type (subsidy, loan, training or insurance), the benefit amount or benefit description and a short description.
4. Click **View Details** or **Read More** to see the full description and the eligibility conditions.

## Check eligibility

Read the **Eligibility** conditions of the scheme before you apply. They differ for each scheme. Sprout can summarise the eligibility rules of a scheme if you ask.

## Apply for a scheme

1. Sign in to your account.
2. Open **Government Portal** and find the scheme.
3. Click **Apply Now**.
4. Your application is created with the status **pending**.

## Track your applications

Scroll to **My Applications** on the same page. Each application shows the scheme name, the date you applied and its status: **pending** (waiting for review), **approved** or **rejected**. If you have not applied for anything yet it says "You haven't applied for any schemes yet".

## Tips

- You can apply to as many schemes as you qualify for.
- If a status looks wrong or you need help with the paperwork, open **Support** and click **Create Ticket**.
