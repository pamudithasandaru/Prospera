# Getting Started: Register, Sign In and Your Profile

## Register a new account

1. Open the website and click **Sign Up** (or go to the Register page).
2. Fill in **Full Name**, **Email Address** and **Phone Number** (for example +94771234567).
3. Choose your **Role**: **Farmer**, **Buyer** or **Agricultural Expert**.
4. Choose your **Language**: English, Sinhala or Tamil.
5. Enter a **Password** and type it again in **Confirm Password**.
6. Click **Sign Up**. You are signed in straight away.

Choose Farmer if you grow crops and want farm tools, loans and schemes. Choose Buyer if you mainly want to buy produce and equipment. Choose Agricultural Expert if you advise farmers and want to publish courses. You cannot choose Admin; admins are appointed by the platform team.

## Sign in

1. Open the Login page.
2. Enter your **Email Address** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign In**.

If your account has been suspended or banned you will see a message and cannot sign in. Contact support if you think this is a mistake.

## Your profile

1. Click your picture at the top right and choose **Profile**.
2. Click **Edit Profile**, change your details, then click **Save Changes**.
3. The profile tabs are **Posts**, **Connections**, **Groups** and **Settings**. Use **Settings** to change your password.
4. The district on your profile is used as the default location on the Weather page.

## Notifications and search

- The bell at the top right shows new likes, comments, follows and messages. Use **Mark all read** to clear them.
- The magnifier opens search for people, posts and hashtags.

## Sign out

Click your picture at the top right and choose **Logout**.
