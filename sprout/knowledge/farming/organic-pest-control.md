# Organic Pest Control Basics

Organic pest control reduces pest damage while protecting soil, water, beneficial insects and the people who work in the field.

## Prevent first

- Rotate crops each season so pests that target one plant family cannot build up.
- Use healthy seed and resistant varieties where available.
- Keep the field clean: remove crop residues and weeds that shelter pests.
- Plant companion crops. Marigold and basil near vegetables can discourage some pests.

## Monitor regularly

Walk the field at least twice a week. Check the underside of leaves, the growing tips and the base of plants. Act when you see pests or damage rising, not after the crop is lost. Yellow sticky traps help to track flying insects such as whiteflies and aphids.

## Natural controls

- Neem solution: spray neem seed extract in the evening to control aphids, whiteflies and caterpillars. Repeat every 7 to 10 days.
- Soap spray: a mild soap solution can control soft-bodied insects such as aphids.
- Bio-pesticides such as Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) work against caterpillars and are safe for most beneficial insects.
- Encourage beneficial insects such as ladybirds and lacewings by avoiding broad-spectrum chemicals.

## Safety

Even natural sprays should be used carefully. Wear gloves and a mask, never spray in strong wind or at midday heat, and follow the label rates. Keep a safe gap between the last spray and harvest.
