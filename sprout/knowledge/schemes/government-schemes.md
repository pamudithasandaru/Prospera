# Government Schemes Listed on Prospera

Prospera's Government section lists subsidies, loans and training programmes for farmers. Amounts below are as listed on the platform. Always confirm the final terms with the issuing authority.

## National Fertilizer Subsidy 2024

- Type: Subsidy (fertiliser)
- Benefit: 50% subsidy on urea and TSP for registered paddy farmers, up to 50,000 per registered hectare.
- Eligibility:
  - Registered paddy farmer
  - Maximum 5 hectares
  - Updated land records

## Agri Machinery Low Interest Loan

- Type: Loan (equipment)
- Benefit: 4% interest loan for tractors and harvesters with a 5-year tenure, up to 2,000,000, with a grace period on repayment.
- Eligibility:
  - Farming income proof
  - Collateral or a guarantor
  - Credit score above 650

## Organic Farming Transition Program

- Type: Training (organic farming)
- Benefit: a 12-week training programme with input grants for farmers moving to organic, worth up to 150,000 as a training stipend and input vouchers.
- Eligibility:
  - Farm size under 10 acres
  - Commitment to organic standards
  - Soil test reports

## How to apply

Open the Government section on Prospera, choose a scheme and submit an application. You can track the status of your applications from the same section.
