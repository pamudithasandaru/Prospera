# Prospera Platform Services

Prospera is a comprehensive agriculture platform for farmers, buyers and agri-entrepreneurs. It combines the following services.

## Farm Management

Register and manage your farms, track crops and activities, and keep farm records in one place.

## Wholesale Market

Browse and post wholesale produce listings and follow market prices, so farmers can sell directly to buyers.

## Marketplace

Buy farm machinery and equipment such as tractors, harvesters, irrigation kits and sprayers from the Prospera Marketplace.

## Learning Hub

Online courses on precision farming, organic pest management, smart irrigation and agri business, taught by experienced instructors.

## Government Schemes

Find subsidies, low interest loans and training programmes, check the eligibility criteria and apply from inside the platform.

## Weather Dashboard

See current weather, forecasts and weather alerts to plan irrigation, spraying and harvesting.

## AI Tools and Plant Disease Prediction

Upload a photo of a plant leaf to get a prediction of the possible disease, using Prospera's machine learning service. Results are guidance only. For severe outbreaks, consult an agriculture officer.

## FinTech

Loan options and a credit score view to help farmers finance equipment and inputs.

## Social Feed and Groups

A farmer community where members can share posts, join groups, and connect with other farmers and experts.

## Support Center

Raise a support ticket for help with your account, orders or any platform issue. Prospera's support team follows up on each ticket. If the assistant cannot answer a question, contact the Support Center.
