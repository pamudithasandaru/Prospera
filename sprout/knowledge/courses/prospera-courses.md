# Prospera Learning Hub Courses

Prospera's Learning Hub offers online courses for farmers and agri-entrepreneurs. Each course lists its level, duration (in hours), instructor and learner rating.

## Precision Farming 101

- Category: Technology
- Level: Intermediate
- Duration: 12 hours
- Instructor: Dr. Nimal Perera
- Rating: 4.6 out of 5 (240 ratings), about 8,200 enrolled students
- What you learn: how to use IoT sensors and data to optimise irrigation and fertiliser use.

## Organic Pest Management

- Category: Pest control
- Level: Beginner
- Duration: 8 hours
- Instructor: Eng. Sajani Silva
- Rating: 4.3 out of 5 (180 ratings), about 5,200 enrolled students
- What you learn: natural pest control methods and bio-pesticides for vegetables.

## Smart Irrigation Design

- Category: Irrigation
- Level: Advanced
- Duration: 10 hours
- Instructor: Prof. Ruwan Dias
- Rating: 4.7 out of 5 (320 ratings), about 6,100 enrolled students
- What you learn: how to design efficient drip and sprinkler systems for different crops.

## Agri Business Fundamentals

- Category: Business
- Level: Beginner
- Duration: 6 hours
- Instructor: Chamari Gunasekara
- Rating: 4.1 out of 5 (140 ratings), about 3,100 enrolled students
- What you learn: pricing, branding and distribution for farm produce.

## Choosing a course

- New to farming technology or business: start with Organic Pest Management or Agri Business Fundamentals (both beginner).
- Already farming and want to save water and inputs: Precision Farming 101 or Smart Irrigation Design.
- The highest rated course is Smart Irrigation Design (4.7).
